User Guide & Instructions

1

Input Sequence: Paste a DNA sequence (A, T, C, G, N). The ideal input is 41 bp with the target Cytosine centered at position 21. Shorter inputs will be padded and longer inputs truncated automatically.

2

Run Prediction: Click Run Prediction to process the sequence through our model, trained specifically on the Mus musculus genome.

3

Review Results: Results appear in the History table. Positive (1) means methylated; Negative (0) means unmethylated.
